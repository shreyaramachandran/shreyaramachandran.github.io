Hello! I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Chicago, advised by [Maanasa Raghavan](https://www.genscape-lab.com) and supported by a TL1 Fellowship in Clinical Research, Biomedical Informatics, and Health Equity (NIH/NCATS). My current project studies the evolution of *Fusobacterium nucleatum* and its association with colorectal cancer. I am also studying for a Master of Public Health at UChicago, concentrating in Epidemiology and Global Health. 

I completed my Ph.D. in Human Genetics at UChicago in 2026. I led a 16S gut microbiome survey of adults across different lifestyles and communities in India and Sri Lanka, and a genomic study of ancient and modern *Streptococcus mutans* from pre-colonial Chile.

I care about combining cross-cultural collaboration and ethical, data-driven science to reduce disease burden and advance global health equity. 

When I'm not doing science, I'm usually practicing my Spanish, solving crossword puzzles, searching for inspiration in my cookbook collection, and exploring the city with my leash-trained cat [Halloumi](/cat/).
