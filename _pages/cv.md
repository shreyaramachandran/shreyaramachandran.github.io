Research Experience
======
* February 2026-current: Postdoctoral Fellow
  * University of Chicago
  * Supported by TL1 Fellowship in Clinical Research, Biomedical Informatics, and Health Equity (NIH/NCATS); studying the evolution of *Fusobacterium nucleatum* and its association with colorectal cancer epidemiology.

* 2018-2026: Ph.D. Candidate
  * University of Chicago
  * Led, designed, and executed 16S gut microbiome survey of South Asian adults; managed a multi-stakeholder project across 10 diverse communities in India and Sri Lanka, building partnerships with local researchers, governments, and community members.
  * Led a genomic study of *Streptococcus mutans* from pre-colonial Chile using ancient and modern metagenomic techniques; coordinated a Spanish-language collaboration with an interdisciplinary international research team.

* 2017-2018: Fulbright-García Robles Researcher
  * Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (Advisor: Maria Ávila-Arcos)
  * Studied the genomic history of Afro-Mexicans and shared results with participants from marginalized communities.

* 2013-2017: Research Assistant
  * Stanford Department of Psychiatry / VA Palo Alto
  * Conducted neurocognitive and sleep assessments; served as Spanish interpreter for participant families.
